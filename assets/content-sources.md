## Instagram

The [official homepage](https://www.snaplii.com/) features 11 community Reels. All 11 original and embed URLs, in homepage order, are recorded in [instagram-posts.json](instagram-posts.json).

The social section presents the three App Store reviews in their own row, followed by a horizontal Instagram gallery. Native lazy-loaded iframes preserve the original creators' attribution. Previous/next buttons, a visible range count and native horizontal scrolling provide access to the full gallery. Original-post URLs remain in the source manifest.

Each portrait card has the same responsive frame dimensions, capped at 360px wide. A clipped media window shows the native creator header and portrait video; Instagram's lower comment area and extra blank space are outside the visible frame. The duplicate card footers and profile link have been removed. Instagram controls embedded content and availability; no creator identity, engagement count, or quote has been invented.
