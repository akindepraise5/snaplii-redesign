Walmart's [official wordmark guidance](https://brandcenter.walmart.com/brand/brand-identity/wordmark)
identifies the wordmark as custom artwork and calls for the Spark to be present
in the same application. Use both original files together, retaining their
proportions; neither has been redrawn or recoloured.

No logo has been generated or recreated with fonts. Raster provider canvases
have not been cropped, resized, or retouched.
