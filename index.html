What exactly is Snaplii Cash?

It's a reward balance earned on qualifying gift card purchases. You can use Snaplii Cash toward eligible orders inside the Snaplii app. It is not cash you can withdraw to a bank account.

How do I use a gift card I buy?

After purchase, the digital gift card appears in your Snaplii wallet. Depending on the merchant, you'll use a barcode, PIN or code online or at the checkout. Check the brand's redemption instructions.

Does Snaplii Cash expire?

Snaplii Cash doesn't expire. Welcome coupons and other special promotions can have separate expiration dates or conditions.

How does the $15 welcome offer work?

When you sign up, you get $15 in coupons to use across your first two orders: one coupon for your first order and one for your second. Like other promotions, the coupons have their own conditions. Terms apply.

What if my gift card doesn't work?

Snaplii has a 120-day Purchase Protection policy for eligible gift card problems. Coverage is subject to terms and exclusions; contact customer support if you experience a problem.

Can I get cash back into my bank account?

No. Snaplii Cash is a balance for eligible purchases within Snaplii and isn't withdrawable as money to your bank account.

Can I shop in Canada and the United States?

Snaplii serves Canada and the United States. The gift cards, reward rates and merchant terms offered can differ by country, so check availability for your region before purchasing.
